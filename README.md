# FNP-Sales-Analysis
Executive Summary – FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard
Overview
The FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard offers a comprehensive view of sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends. It analyzes a total of 1000 orders generating ₹5,86,176 in revenue. The key performance metrics, visualized across multiple dimensions, help stakeholders make data-driven decisions regarding marketing, inventory, and operations.

Key Performance Metrics
Total Orders: 1000

Total Revenue: ₹5,86,176

Average Days between Order and Delivery: ~5.72 days

Average Customer Spend: ₹4,652.19

Revenue Insights
Top Products by Revenue:

Mugam Set, Decorative Item, and Expression Gift lead the chart, indicating strong consumer preference.

These top 10 products contribute significantly to the total revenue and should be prioritized in marketing and stock management.

Revenue by Category:

Soft Toys is the highest-earning category (₹3,00,000+), followed by Sweets and Colors.

Categories like Mugs and Raksha Bandhan have relatively lower revenue, indicating potential areas for promotional boosts.

Revenue by Month:

Revenue peaks in February and December, aligning with Valentine's Day and holiday season purchases.

Notable dips in March and May suggest potential gaps in campaign planning or seasonal demand.

Revenue by Occasion:

Anniversary, Valentine’s Day, and Raksha Bandhan are high-revenue occasions.

Diwali and Birthday also contribute consistently, but there's room to increase engagement during these events.

Revenue by Hour (Order Time):

Orders spike at 10 AM–12 PM and 5 PM–7 PM, suggesting optimal customer engagement hours for marketing pushes and website performance optimization.

Customer and Geographic Insights
Top 10 Cities by Orders:

Cities like Kolkata, Kota, Trichy, and Meerut show the highest number of orders.

Emerging Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities are significant contributors and should be part of targeted marketing strategies.

Operational Insights
The average delivery time of 5.72 days suggests a need to optimize logistics for faster fulfillment.

Monitoring delivery delays and correlating them with customer satisfaction or refund rates would be beneficial.

Recommendations
Product Strategy:

Prioritize inventory and advertising for high-performing products like Mugam Sets and Soft Toys.

Consider bundling low-performing items with top sellers.

Marketing Campaigns:

Boost promotions during underperforming months (March, May).

Leverage high-revenue occasions with personalized campaigns.

Geographic Focus:

Expand outreach and logistics in high-performing Tier-2 cities.

Use local festivals or occasions to personalize offerings.

Customer Retention:

With high average spend per customer (~₹4,652), loyalty programs and offers for repeat buyers can be highly effective.

Logistics Optimization:

Explore faster delivery options to reduce the average 5.72-day delivery time, enhancing customer satisfaction.

Conclusion
This dashboard gives actionable insights into FNP’s sales performance across products, categories, locations, and time periods. By capitalizing on high-performing segments and addressing operational inefficiencies, FNP can drive higher customer satisfaction and revenue growth.
